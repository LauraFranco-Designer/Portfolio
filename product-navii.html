Empowering through customization

System of tools to present Navii prosthetcive cover to Users and CPOs

The project introduces a tailored design system to enhance the customization experience of the NAVii® protective knee cover for transfemoral prostheses. By integrating user insights and clinical feedback, the system enables meaningful personalization through a selection of colors and sizes. It supports users in expressing identity and comfort while providing CPOs with tools to guide the decision-making process effectively. The outcome encourages greater device acceptance and psychological well-being

Developed in collaboration with Össur this project focuses on optimizing the selection journey of the NAVii® knee cover. Through qualitative research and co-design methods, the project identifies the needs of both users and Certified Prosthetist/Orthotists. The final solution offers a series of tested concepts that streamline the customization experience, align with Össur’s service strategy, and foster a more supportive and engaging interaction between patients and clinicians

Research Process

The research began with the goal of mapping the entire system involving end users, CPOs, Össur, and key stakeholders to understand the dynamics through which customization options are introduced. This overview also highlights how interactions occur within the system, revealing a current lack of direct communication between Össur and the end users. Instead, this communication is mediated through the CPOs, who are a fundamental link in the chain, something that must be considered when designing future products and strategies

The research then moved on to mapping the end user journey from the moment of limb amputation to full recovery. It was necessary to plot two separate journeys: one for individuals who underwent amputation due to trauma (green line in the graph), and another for those whose amputation resulted from a chronic condition (yellow line). This distinction ensured that no key touchpoints, especially those unique to each path, were overlooked in the analysis

The second journey mapped focused on outlining the acquisition process we expect end users to go through when selecting the Navii Knee It highlights where interactions between the users and the CPO are likely to take place throughout the decision-making path.

An extra layer of information was then added on top of this journey path, showing where customization options could be introduced. It defines the opportunities, concerns, and possible channels for each phase of the process

Furthermore, different modalities of sales and delivery strategies were explored and analyzed, highlighting the respective opportunities and challenges for all the main actors in the system (End Users, CPOs, and Össur)
